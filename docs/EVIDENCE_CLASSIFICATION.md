# Evidence Classification

Every evidence record and public metric should display an evidence classification badge.

## Classifications

### Reported

Directly disclosed by the company through trusted first-party evidence such as SEC filings, annual reports, quarterly reports, earnings calls, or investor relations materials.

### Estimated

Based on third-party analysis or trusted external reporting. Estimated evidence must preserve source lineage and should not be treated as directly disclosed company data.

### Derived

Calculated by APIP from approved input metrics. ROI is a common derived metric because APIP computes it from revenue and spend inputs.

### Validated

Approved by a human reviewer. Validated records can be published by the Publisher Agent.

## Display Rules

Classification badges appear on:

- Public metrics.
- Trust Center evidence records.
- Admin research and approval queues.
- Source lineage views.
- Validation reports.

## Lifecycle

Collected evidence can begin as `Reported`, `Estimated`, or `Derived`. Reviewer approval changes the record classification to `Validated` before publishing.
